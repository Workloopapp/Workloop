Title: [iOS beta] Workloop — clients, bookings and money for a business of one

I'm building Workloop for people who work for themselves, by themselves. The
first version focuses on solo service businesses: keeping client details,
bookings, tasks and income/expenses connected on your phone.

I'd like feedback from people who book work for clients, particularly cleaners,
mobile valeters/detailers, independent beauty professionals and tutors. Other

The main thing I want to test is whether the daily dashboard makes the next
action clear. A small test would be:

1. Add a client (sample details are fine).
2. Create a booking.
3. Record a payment and find it again.

Where did you have to stop and work out what to do? What information did you
expect to see but couldn't find?
