# Expense records and tax estimate — 8 September 2026

This phase extends the existing expense editor and Money workflow. It does not replace recorded cash profit with a tax figure.

## Delivered source

- Expense receipts: attach an original JPEG, PNG or PDF selected through the existing device file picker, up to 10 MB. New expenses use a stable client UUID so retrying a lost create response does not duplicate the expense. If attachment fails after expense save, the editor retains the expense ID and explains what needs retrying. Existing originals can be exported or removed.
- Business mileage: date, business purpose/route, consistent vehicle identity/type and integer hundredths of a mile; create, edit, delete and CSV export. Mileage does not create a cash expense or change the Money profit chart.
- `TaxEstimateScreen`: saved, manually reviewed annual input amounts, with recorded tax-year cash receipts and expenses shown separately for reconciliation. New invoice cash receipts use their actual movement dates through `Payment.receivedAmountBetween`.
- Tax arithmetic uses integer pence and version `uk-ewni-2026-27-v1`. Supported year is 6 April 2026–5 April 2027, for eligible sole traders in England, Wales or Northern Ireland. The screen explicitly excludes Scotland, companies, other taxable income, VAT, special allowances/reliefs, student loans, benefit charges, CIS, carried losses, capital allowances and National Insurance exemptions. Eligibility is reconfirmed after reopening.
- Vehicle deductions select actual allowable vehicle costs OR logged mileage. The user confirms eligible consistent mileage treatment and excludes running/ownership costs from other expenses. Car/van mileage shares one 10,000-mile band across the trade at 55p; further miles use 25p, motorcycles 24p.
- Result: taxable profit, tapered personal allowance, Income Tax, Class 4 National Insurance, annual liability, payments already allocated to this year and reserve shortfall. Indicative advance payments on account and dates are separate from the annual reserve. Voluntary Class 2 and HMRC filing are excluded. A text report can be shared.

## Privacy and security

Migration `20260908180227_expense_receipts_mileage_tax_estimates.sql` adds `expense_receipts`, `mileage_entries` and `workspace_tax_estimates`, plus a private Storage bucket. Existing workspace membership and MFA policy apply; receipt paths are tied to the exact expense/workspace. File limits are bucket configuration, because Storage upload preflight does not contain final object-size metadata.

Receipt metadata cannot be updated. Expense deletion removes object bytes and receipt metadata first; a deferred foreign key prevents premature expense deletion. Account completion removes every receipt object under the workspace prefix before relational cascades. Both authenticated upload preflight and privileged Storage completion reject uploads during requested/processing deletion, and completion rejects absent receipt metadata. The final-write trigger is narrowly limited to `expense-receipts`; Storage's own failure path deletes failed uploaded versions.

Tax editors remount by workspace key; saved-input providers are keyed by workspace. Mileage and expense editors pin their opening workspace. Receipt reads include workspace filters, and exports/deletes recheck workspace after asynchronous steps.

Workspace export format 3 includes document data, mileage, tax inputs, receipt metadata and base64 original receipt bytes. Mobile receipt export is bounded to 20 MB total before downloading and while accumulating. Larger exports fail explicitly without silently producing a partial file; the UI directs the owner to individual original exports or support for an archive.

The tax screen starts those inputs from existing journeys and requires a whole-year review. The forecast cannot be lower than recorded business miles. Residence and vehicle-method controls are disabled during a save, and changed input revisions cannot acquire a stale asynchronous result. Tax rates and the combined 10,000-mile car/van band are unchanged and were rechecked against the primary HMRC guidance linked above.

Recorded income can now be explicitly copied as a starting figure, using actual receipt movements rather than invoiced totals. The owner still reviews future income, outside records and allowable costs; candidate expenses are not copied as automatically allowable. Saved company/other business structure stops the sole-trader shortcut; tax residence is never inferred from an address. Tax conditions use progressive disclosure, with scope and exclusions retained. Results and long amounts adapt to narrow screens and enlarged text. Negative pence below one pound now retain their minus sign.

## Native receipt capture and paid-date correction

The device review showed that the Files-only flow required an owner to move a receipt photo into Files first. The receipt section now offers **Take photo**, **Photo library**, and **Choose file** through the shared Workloop bottom sheet. This is capture and attachment only; receipt details remain manually entered.

The official Flutter [`image_picker` 1.2.3 package](https://pub.dev/packages/image_picker) supplies the native camera/library routes. Its Dart 3.10 minimum is reflected in `pubspec.yaml`; the configured Flutter 3.44.8 toolchain satisfies it. Dependency resolution added the picker and its platform implementations/file-selector dependencies, without upgrading existing packages. Existing iOS camera/photo purpose strings now explain receipt capture. Android needs no additional manifest permissions for this plugin flow.

Camera/library access starts only after the owner selects that action. Individual photo selection sets `requestFullMetadata: false`. The plugin is asked for at most 4096 pixels on each dimension at quality 95, and returned bytes must be a JPEG/PNG of at most 10 MiB. The app identifies the actual format, gives the saved copy the correct extension, and provides an explicit fallback if HEIC remains unsupported. Photos are described as high-quality copies because native conversion or resizing can change them; Files imports preserve selected JPEG/PNG/PDF bytes. Subsequent export exports exactly the saved attachment, including a converted photo copy where applicable.

Cancellation keeps any pending receipt. Denied/restricted access and unavailable camera results offer Settings/retry or another source. A single busy state covers chooser and native picker: duplicate capture, expense save and draft exit are blocked until completion. The active workspace is observed and must be resolved and unchanged when a result returns; navigation disposal ignores late callbacks. Android activity-loss retrieval does not attach an unscoped result to a new business/expense; it asks the owner to choose again. These choices follow the plugin's [platform guidance](https://pub.dev/packages/image_picker#android) without persisting a new draft or account-linking scheme.

Expense **Date paid** now stops at the shared current business day. Existing future-dated records remain unchanged and visible until the owner explicitly corrects them; attempting to save shows an inline explanation, and cancelling the date picker preserves the legacy date. Tax reference figures exclude and flag future-dated expenses and cash receipt movements. Today's expenses and receipts through the current instant remain included; future income and spending belong in reviewed annual forecasts.

## Receipt text extraction and reviewed autofill

The next receipt phase replaces the earlier capture-only/manual-entry limit above. Selecting a receipt now starts on-device reading and opens **Review receipt details** before the expense changes. The owner can view the full image/PDF, correct the suggestions, apply selected fields, or keep the existing details. Amount/date/supplier/reference suggestions reuse the existing expense fields: supplier and reference append to the note, and category remains an owner choice. Existing amounts, explicitly chosen paid dates and notes are not selected for replacement by default. Changes typed while reading are also preserved unless the owner explicitly selects a replacement. Applying a review does not save the expense.

The Dart bridge uses `workloop/receipt_text` with only the selected JPEG/PNG/PDF bytes and MIME type. Recognized text remains transient in memory; the app does not send it to a cloud OCR service, persist raw OCR text, or write a new database record. The existing receipt attachment is uploaded only through the existing private receipt save path. The native contract returns page counts and a truncation flag, allowing the review to disclose a five-page scan limit and truncated text separately. Partial scans do not preselect amount/date. The Dart boundary additionally bounds recognized text to 100,000 characters.

The pure parser proposes only a single consistent labelled total, excluding subtotals, tax totals, savings, excluded-VAT totals, cash and change. Conflicting totals require manual review rather than choosing the largest value. Parenthesized, minus-signed, CR-marked and explicitly refunded/credited totals suppress a positive purchase suggestion for the whole receipt; ordinary credit-card payment labels remain supported. Amounts use integer pence. Foreign/mixed currencies cannot be applied as a GBP amount; an unidentified currency requires explicit GBP confirmation. Ambiguous numeric dates, future dates, invoice-like text and credit/refund notes receive specific explanations. Paid dates remain bounded by the current business day. These suggestions do not establish payment, tax allowability or receipt authenticity.

Reading can be cancelled without discarding the attachment. Unreadable/locked documents and unavailable native OCR offer manual entry. Expense save/capture/exit remain guarded during reading and review; the progress animation stops once review opens. A request or review is revoked after an account/business change, including switching away and back. Starting another read also requires the editor’s original business, so a retained old callback cannot reopen its receipt under another business. The full receipt view reuses the shared private document viewer and its identity and image-cache safeguards.
